PharmaPass privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Medicheck Co ("we") handles data in PharmaPass: GCC Exam Prep, an Android exam-preparation app for pharmacist licensing exams. The app has no accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. There are no ads and no analytics.

Data that stays on your device

Your answers, flashcard progress, saved questions, mock exam results, exam date, study goal and reminder settings are stored only on your phone. Study reminders are created on your phone, and the app asks your permission before showing notifications.

Data sent to our server

Data leaves your phone only when you use one of these features:

  • AI tutor. We receive the practice question, the answer you chose, your typed message, whether you have Premium, and a random install ID created by the app.
  • Problem reports. We receive the question ID, the reason, your optional comment, the AI answer being reported, the app version and the install ID.

The install ID is not linked to your name, your Google account or your device's hardware. We use it to apply the daily AI tutor limit and to connect your records so they can be deleted. Like any web server, ours also receives your IP address with each request. We use it to block abuse, and it appears in short-lived server logs.

How we use data

We use this data to answer your tutor questions, apply usage limits, control costs, prevent abuse and fix reported problems. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes data for us

  • OpenAI receives the question and your message through its API to write tutor answers. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train its models by default. Please don't type personal or patient information into the tutor.
  • Render hosts our server and Neon hosts our database, both in Frankfurt, EU.
  • Google Play processes Premium purchases, which are one-time payments. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to that data.

We don't share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Security

All data sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to the server and database is restricted to those who run the service.

How long we keep data

  • Tutor messages are not saved in our database after the answer is written.
  • Daily limit and usage records (install ID, question ID, token counts and cost) are kept for cost control until you delete your data. Usage records then stay without the install ID, so they can't be connected to your install.
  • Problem reports are kept as part of our content history until you delete your data.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete my data. This erases everything on your device, deletes your problem reports and daily limit records from our server, and removes the install ID from usage records. Uninstalling the app also erases the on-device data, but does not remove data from our server. If you've already uninstalled the app, email us to ask about your data.

Children

PharmaPass is intended for adult pharmacy graduates and professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
